# Rose Glass Recovery - Test Transcript
## Counselor Session with Pattern Analysis Notes

**Session Date:** January 25, 2026  
**Client:** "Marcus" (pseudonym), 34, 18 months in recovery  
**Setting:** Outpatient addiction counseling, weekly check-in  
**Duration:** ~10 minutes  

---

## PRE-SESSION COUNSELOR NOTES

Marcus has been in recovery for 18 months. Usually engaged, attends meetings regularly. 
Last two sessions felt "off" - he's saying the right things but something's different.
Today I want to gently explore what's underneath the surface.

---

## TRANSCRIPT

### [0:00] Opening

**COUNSELOR:** Hey Marcus, good to see you. How's your week been?

**MARCUS:** Good, good. Everything's fine. Really solid actually.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** That "really" feels like emphasis where none was needed. 
> Filing that away.

---

### [1:30] Initial Check-in

**COUNSELOR:** Glad to hear it. What's been solid about it?

**MARCUS:** I don't know, just... under control, you know? I've got my routine down. Work, meetings, home. Same thing every day. It's fine.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** "Under control" + "same thing every day" + "fine" again. 
> He's describing stability but the energy feels flat. No spark when he talks about his life.
> Also noticed he didn't mention anyone else - just locations.

---

### [2:45] Probing Gently

**COUNSELOR:** How are things at home with Sarah?

**MARCUS:** She's... she's good. Busy with her stuff. We're both just doing our own thing, I guess. It works.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** "Her stuff" and "our own thing" - that's separation language. 
> Last month he said "we" a lot. Now it's all parallel lives.
> "It works" is not the same as "we're good."

---

### [3:30] Family Dynamics

**COUNSELOR:** And the kids?

**MARCUS:** They're at that age where dad's not cool anymore, right? Always on their phones. Emma barely looks up when I come home.

*[Slight pause, Marcus looks away]*

**MARCUS:** But that's normal. All parents deal with that.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** There it is. That pause before the rationalization.
> "Barely looks up when I come home" - he felt something there.
> The normalization came quick, like a reflex.

---

### [4:45] Exploring Isolation

**COUNSELOR:** That can be hard. Feeling like you're not connecting.

**MARCUS:** I mean, it's not... I don't need connection from a teenager, you know? I've got my meetings. I've got my sponsor. All that.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** "I don't need" - denial pattern.
> He listed support structures but with no warmth. Checking boxes.
> When was the last time he actually called his sponsor?

---

### [5:30] Digging Deeper

**COUNSELOR:** When did you last talk to Ray?

**MARCUS:** *[Brief hesitation]* Couple weeks ago. Maybe three. He's been busy. I've been busy. You know how it is.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** Three weeks without sponsor contact. That's a shift.
> The excuse is mutual - "he's busy, I'm busy" - spreading responsibility.
> This is the f-dimension declining and he doesn't see it.

---

### [6:15] The Contradiction

**COUNSELOR:** Marcus, you said everything's under control. But you also haven't talked to Ray in three weeks, and you're describing home like... parallel lives.

**MARCUS:** *[Defensive posture]* I'm not using. I go to work. I go to meetings. I haven't missed a single one. What more do you want?

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** The checklist defense. Classic performance.
> Notice he went to behavior, not experience. 
> "What more do you want" - frustration leaking through.
> This is actually more authentic than "everything's fine."

---

### [7:00] Creating Safety

**COUNSELOR:** I'm not doubting your commitment, Marcus. I'm wondering how you're actually feeling. Not what you're doing. How you're doing.

*[Long silence - maybe 10 seconds]*

**MARCUS:** *[Quieter]* Tired. I'm tired.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** There. That's real.
> The silence let him drop the performance.
> "Tired" is the first genuine statement of the session.

---

### [7:45] Opening Up

**COUNSELOR:** Tell me about tired.

**MARCUS:** I do everything right. Every single day. And nobody... 

*[Stops, collects himself]*

**MARCUS:** My wife looks at me like she's waiting for me to screw up. My kids don't see me. At meetings, everyone's got their problems, nobody asks about mine. I just... I'm doing this alone. All of it.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** Now we're somewhere real.
> "Nobody" - "alone" - "don't see me" - isolation language flooding in.
> He's been holding this. Maybe for weeks.
> The performance of recovery is exhausting him.

---

### [8:30] Validation

**COUNSELOR:** That sounds incredibly lonely.

**MARCUS:** *[Eyes wet]* I thought it would feel different by now. Eighteen months and I still feel like I'm on probation. Like one wrong move and it all falls apart.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** "Probation" - not belonging. Low f-dimension.
> "One wrong move" - hypervigilance. Living in threat.
> He's not connected to his recovery, he's performing it.

---

### [9:15] Closing

**COUNSELOR:** Marcus, what you just said - that's important. That feeling of doing everything right and still being on the outside. Can we talk more about that next week?

**MARCUS:** *[Nods]* Yeah. Yeah, maybe we should.

**COUNSELOR:** And between now and then - could you call Ray? Not because you have to. Just to hear a friendly voice.

**MARCUS:** *[Small smile]* I'll think about it.

> **COUNSELOR'S INTERNAL:** Small progress. The mask slipped.
> He let me see the isolation underneath the performance.
> Next session we need to work on authentic connection, not just meeting attendance.
> Watch this one. He's more fragile than he's showing.

## WHAT ROSE GLASS SHOULD REVEAL

The counselor in this transcript notices patterns intuitively:
- Performance vs authenticity
- Isolation language creeping in
- Contradiction between "fine" and the underlying exhaustion
- Social connection declining (f-dimension)
- The break when real emotion surfaces

**Rose Glass makes these intuitions visible** without replacing clinical judgment.

When Input 1 shows "stable" but Input 4 shows "concern" - that's the trajectory. 
That's what the counselor felt but couldn't quantify.

Translation, not measurement. Validation, not replacement.
